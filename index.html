About

I work on optimization and machine learning, with a focus on adaptive first-order methods and optimizers for neural networks.

I am interested in how optimization methods can adapt to unknown problem parameters, and how gradient history can help us train neural networks.

I studied Data Science and Big Data Technology at Shanghai University of Finance and Economics (2022–2026). I work with Qi Deng at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Yinyu Ye at Stanford University.
