Haifeng Xu

I work on optimization and machine learning. I am particularly interested in first-order methods that adapt to unknown problem parameters, and in how gradient history can help train neural networks.

I studied Data Science and Big Data Technology at Shanghai University of Finance and Economics (2022–2026). I work with Qi Deng at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Yinyu Ye at Stanford University.
