Research interests

Theory and practice
01 /

Optimization without hand tuning

How can first-order methods adapt to unknown distance and smoothness parameters while retaining strong convergence guarantees? My work studies accelerated, tuning-free methods for convex and stochastic optimization.

NeurIPS 2025 paper ↗
02 /

Learning from gradient history

Old gradients can remain useful thousands of steps later, and their value varies across training examples. I study how optimizers can retain that information while responding to recent changes in training.

Hybrid Momentum preprint ↗

Ongoing work. A further study on tuning-free optimization is complete; the manuscript is being polished for journal submission. Additional projects are in progress.
