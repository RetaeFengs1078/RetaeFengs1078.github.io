I am an undergraduate student at the Shanghai University of Finance and Economics (SUFE), studying Data Science and Big Data Technology in the Pilot Class of the Research Institute for Interdisciplinary Science.

My research interests include first-order optimization methods, online algorithms, machine learning, and game theory. I am currently working on tuning-free optimization methods and schedule-free methods with preconditioning.

## Research

### Tuning-free First Order Methods
*January 2025 - Present, Shanghai Jiao Tong University*
Mentored by Prof. Qi Deng.

- Developed an accelerated distance-adaptive method with optimal anytime convergence rates for Holder-smooth problems, without prior knowledge of smoothness parameters or explicit parameter tuning.
- Extended the method to linearly constrained convex problems and stochastic gradients.
- This work resulted in a NeurIPS 2025 paper; an extension manuscript is in preparation.

### Schedule-free Method and Preconditioner
*September 2025 - Present, Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Stanford University*
Mentored by Prof. Qi Deng and Prof. Yinyu Ye.

- Studying advantages of operator norms in matrix optimization.
- Applying online scaling methods to improve upon schedule-free approaches.

### Online Robust Mechanism on Cooperative Games
*September 2024 - February 2025, Shanghai University of Finance and Economics*
Mentored by Prof. Hu Fu and Prof. Zhihao Gavin Tang.

- Constructed a counterexample showing that no mechanism can surpass an O(1/k) approximation rate.
- Proved that, with partial information about the value function, linear programming can design a complete mechanism.
