This paper studies parameter-free first-order methods for convex optimization problems with Holder-smooth objectives. We propose an accelerated distance-adaptive method that achieves optimal anytime convergence rates without requiring prior knowledge of smoothness parameters or explicit parameter tuning. The approach also removes the need to specify a target accuracy in advance and extends to convex stochastic optimization. *Equal contribution.*
